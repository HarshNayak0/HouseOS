## Keep these in your password manager

## Backups
Your database is backed up every Sunday, encrypted with your passphrase, and kept for 8 weeks. Photos and
PDFs aren't in the backup.

## Your data
It lives in your accounts. The vault and your calendars and notes are never sent to an AI service.
The vault protects against a leaked database or backup, but it isn't end-to-end encrypted — please don't
store bank passwords there.
