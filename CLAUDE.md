- The full spec is in `SPEC.md`. Read it at the start of every session.
- The 3D house is defined by `reference/veedu-prototype.html`. Port it; don't redesign it.
- **Cost rule:** the app must stay free to run (target $0/month, ceiling ~$2/month). Ask before adding any paid service, asset, font or domain.
- Work one phase at a time. Commit and push at the end of each phase, then stop and tell me how to test it on my phone.
- For anything only I can do (account setup, console settings, secrets), give numbered click-by-click steps and wait for me.
- Never send vault, notes or calendar data to an AI provider.
- Store times in UTC; display in America/Toronto.
- Keep `README.md` and `docs/DECISIONS.md` up to date.
